How it works

The bot taught itself by playing about 13.9 million games against itself. Its evaluation is an n-tuple network: four 6-cell patterns, each looked up in all 8 rotations and reflections of the board, trained with temporal-difference learning. Here it plays with expectimax search, depth 2: for each move it averages over the tiles that could spawn, two layers deep, and picks the move the network expects to be best.

Each game gets a fresh random seed. The server computes it and streams every move; your browser checks every board against the rules and replays it at a fixed pace.

Measured at depth 2:

  • 90.5% of 3,000 evaluation games reach 16384
  • 320.8k mean score over those games
  • 0.124 ms per move on one Apple M4 core
